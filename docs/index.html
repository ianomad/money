How we compare: base vs. top APY, checks, caveats

BEHIND THE NUMBERS

Read the rate.
Then the terms.

Base vs. top

Coverage is curated across US providers; CDs, targeted offers, and cash signup bonuses are excluded. Base is the standard rate for the stated tier. Top is the highest verified public offer for the listed account, including referral boosts, promotions, and qualifying tiers. Caps, fees, and eligibility appear alongside it; boosts are combined only where the provider permits.

Checked, not streamed

This is a reviewed snapshot of official provider pages, checked 2026-10-13. Rates are rechecked daily against those pages, not streamed live. Every listing links to its sources and provider date when available.

APY isn’t take-home yield

APY includes compounding. Membership costs, taxes, balance caps, and temporary boosts affect what you earn. Rates are variable; a three-month promotion is not a one-year return.

What FDIC coverage means

FDIC coverage is generally $250,000 per depositor, per insured bank, per ownership category. Deposits held directly or through different apps at the same bank count together. Pass-through coverage requires program conditions to be met. It covers insured bank failure, not a fintech platform’s failure or crypto losses. FDIC coverage rules ↗
