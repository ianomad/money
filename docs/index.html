BEHIND THE NUMBERS

Read the rate.
Then the terms.

Base vs. listed

Base is the standard rate. Listed includes the featured conditional or promotional rate where shown. It is not an exhaustive maximum; optional promotions may be excluded.

Checked, not streamed

This is a manually reviewed snapshot of official provider pages, checked 2026-10-03. Rates do not refresh automatically. Every listing links to its sources and provider date when available.

APY isn’t take-home yield

APY includes compounding. Membership costs, taxes, balance caps, and temporary boosts affect what you earn. Rates are variable; a three-month promotion is not a one-year return.

Compare within categories

Cash and crypto stay separate. Deposit insurance eligibility depends on the institution, ownership, and program terms. Crypto rewards are not FDIC-insured deposits.
