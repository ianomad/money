Details ›

Deposit APY reported by Aave’s public API (the data behind Aave Pro) at 2026-10-09 14:34 UTC. This is one USDC market, not a protocol-wide rate. At this snapshot about 84% of the market’s supplied USDC was borrowed, which is what pushed the deposit rate up; it can fall quickly as borrowers repay or new deposits arrive. Rates change with market utilisation and governance parameters; other assets, networks, and markets have different yields. Network fees reduce net returns. Withdrawals depend on available liquidity and, if borrowing, collateral requirements.
