How these numbers work

Figures refresh every morning from FRED (Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis), which republishes Freddie Mac, EIA, Treasury, Federal Reserve and S&P data. Most lag by a day or more; weekly series by up to a week. A number older than 14 days is marked “may be out of date”. The 30-day change compares the latest reading with the one about a month earlier. Bitcoin is a CoinGecko snapshot.

Freddie Mac’s weekly survey average for a 30-year fixed loan. The monthly figure is the standard amortization formula on $400,000 over 360 months at 7.32%; a year ago the survey read 6.27%.

EIA weekly retail price for regular gasoline, all US formulations. Fill-up = 15 gallons × the average price (Oct 26, 2026) and × the price a year earlier ($3.02).

West Texas Intermediate spot price, $103.37 a barrel on Oct 27, 2026, vs $62.13 a year earlier.

Effective federal funds rate from the New York Fed. The savings figure is the highest base rate among the cash accounts on our Savings page, from each provider’s own site.

Market yield on 10-year US Treasury notes, constant maturity. A year ago it was 4.01%.

Index level at the close on Oct 28, 2026, vs 6,891 a year earlier. Price only, no dividends.

Nominal broad dollar index against major trade partners’ currencies (Jan 2006 = 100).
