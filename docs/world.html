MISSED OPPORTUNITY · KYRGYZSTAN

$500,000 in a som deposit, October 2023 → today

Converted at the NBKR rate of 88.92 KGS/USD on 2023-10-12, compounded yearly for 3 years, converted back at today’s 87.47. The som moved -1.6% against the dollar, so the currency barely hurt. We don’t have a verified 2023 rate sheet yet, so these are scenarios, not what a bank actually paid then. Kyrgyz rates on the table today top out at 14% (14.66% effective at ABank). The US baseline is 4.27% for 3 years = $566,824.

The insurance cap is 1M som (about $11,433), so $500k is almost all uninsured unless you spread it over many banks. Other risks: devaluation, rules on moving money out of the country, and AML checks on large inbound wires. US taxpayers owe US tax on foreign interest and must file FBAR/FATCA reports.
