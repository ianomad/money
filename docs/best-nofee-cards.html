HOW TO PICK

Most people should take a flat 2% no-fee card and stop optimizing.

  • Want zero homework: Citi Double Cash, SoFi Unlimited 2%, Wells Fargo Active Cash — same 2%, $0 fee.
  • Spend over ~$6,000/yr and already want Gold: Robinhood Gold Card’s 3% nets $660 here vs $480 free.
  • Dining + groceries are most of your card spend: re-run the category table with your mix — Savor / Altitude Go can flip ahead of flat 2% when those buckets are fat.
  • Ignore sign-up bonuses here: they are one-time. This page ranks the ongoing earn rate only.
