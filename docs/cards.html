How we count the value

Points and miles counted at 1¢ each (what Chase, Amex, Capital One and Bilt points are worth as cash or in the issuer's travel portal without a boost). Travel transfers can be worth more; we don't assume it. Credits and perks are not counted, because their value depends on whether you'd spend that money anyway.

Default spending: dining $300, groceries $400, gas $150, travel $150, streaming $30, everything else $970. Signup bonuses are listed but not counted, since they only pay once. Caps are applied (Amex Gold, Blue Cash Preferred). Rent cards are compared on everyday spend; their edge is earning on rent with no fee.
