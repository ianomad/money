How it works

Points and miles counted at 1¢ each (what Chase, Amex, Capital One and Bilt points are worth as cash or in the issuer's travel portal without a boost). Travel transfers can be worth more; we don't assume it. Credits and perks are not counted, because their value depends on whether you'd spend that money anyway.

Default spending: dining $300, groceries $400, gas $150, travel $150, streaming $30, everything else $970. An ongoing year leaves signup bonuses out, since they pay once. Turn on the first-year switch to add a bonus, counted only if your spending over its window meets the requirement. Caps are applied (Amex Gold, Blue Cash Preferred and Everyday, Altitude Go). Rotating 5% categories (Discover) and portal-only rates (5x Chase Travel and similar) are not counted. Rent cards are compared on everyday spend.

Card terms were checked against issuer pages on Oct 25, 2026; cards marked "Re-checked" were checked again later. Terms change often, so confirm on the issuer page before you apply.
